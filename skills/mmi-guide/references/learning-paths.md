# Learning paths

Start with a question, and check `routes.json` for package availability. A planned chapter is not yet usable. Give only the prerequisite needed for the selected equation. The five parts below are the book's own grouping; chapters not yet built are listed by title only.

| Goal | Suggested path |
|---|---|
| Judge what a person-plus-machine pair adds | 1 (compare with the best alternative), 2 (weights and shared errors), then 25 when built |
| Weigh unreliable sources | 2 (precision weights, correlated errors, one source or two) |
| Understand memory and attention | 6 (stored patterns, energy, softmax as attention); 5 and 7 when built |
| Part I: Making Sense of the World | 2 to 5 |
| Part II: Turning Experience into Knowledge | 6 to 12 |
| Part III: Giving Knowledge a Purpose | 13 to 20 |
| Part IV: Intelligence Working with Intelligence | 21 to 25 |
| Part V: From Abilities to Minds | 26 to 34 |

For a reader uncomfortable with algebra, begin with the pictures: bars of weights, a shifting curve, a grid of units. Introduce a symbol only when it is needed to read the next figure.

For an advanced reader, open the equation card's assumptions and discuss whether the result is an identity, a bound, an approximation or a simulation. Do not make the reader repeat elementary material they already understand.
