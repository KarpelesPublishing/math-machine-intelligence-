How to use the companion

Open a chapter, make a prediction, move a slider and press "Show what happens". The equations, symbols and the full hand calculation sit under each figure. Reading a saved notebook needs no programming.

The master skill mmi-guide and the chapter skills help apply a chapter's mathematics to your own question. They run in an assistant that supports SKILL.md packages.

Examples use book values or disclosed illustrative parameters. A simulation is not a measurement, and for a brain the mathematics is a model: a close fit to behavior does not by itself show how neurons produce it. Nothing here is clinical or treatment advice.
